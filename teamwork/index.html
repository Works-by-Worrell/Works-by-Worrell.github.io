Practical Execution

Three Habits for Cross-Team Teamwork

1

Share the "Why" Over Just the "What"

Business context shapes better software design; architectural clarity prevents unexpected timeline friction.

"Communicate everything you possibly can to your partners. The more they know, the more they'll understand. The more they understand, the more they'll care. And once they care, there's no stopping them." — Mr. Sam (Rule #4)
2

Blameless Problem Solving

When feeds fail or anomalies hit: focus on unblocking immediately, analyzing the root cause, and building resilience together.

3

Focus on the Frontline Specialist

Our true end user is the specialist standing at the counter trying to solve a customer's urgent problem.

"The folks on the front lines are the only ones who really know what's going on out there." — Mr. Sam
